GOH Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

1. Introduction

GOH Hub is a customer service messaging platform used to help manage customer conversations, enquiries and operational communication through WhatsApp and related business systems.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when customers communicate with us through GOH Hub.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may process information such as:

  • Name or WhatsApp profile name
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp messages sent to our business
  • Shipment, booking or receiving information provided by you
  • Customer service conversation history
  • Message timestamps and delivery information
  • Technical information required to operate the messaging service

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to customer enquiries
  • Provide customer service and operational support
  • Route conversations to the appropriate customer service staff
  • Track unresolved or overdue enquiries
  • Provide shipment, booking or warehouse-related assistance
  • Maintain customer service records
  • Improve our service and messaging workflow
  • Operate automated customer support features where applicable

4. WhatsApp and Meta

GOH Hub may use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. Messages sent through WhatsApp may also be processed according to WhatsApp and Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customer personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers and technology platforms that are necessary to operate GOH Hub, including messaging, database, hosting and infrastructure providers.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or regulatory authorities.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration or disclosure. Access to customer conversations is limited to authorized personnel and systems used for customer service operations.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, operational, legal, security and business record purposes.

8. Your Rights and Data Requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with your customer service conversations, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
